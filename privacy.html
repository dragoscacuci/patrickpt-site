Privacy Policy

Patrick PT for iPhone · Last updated 16 November 2026

In short: Patrick PT does not collect, sell or share any data. Your client list stays on your iPhone and in your own private iCloud. The developer never receives it.

What the app stores

To work, Patrick PT saves the following on your device and, if iCloud is on, in your private iCloud database (managed by Apple under your Apple Account):

  • Your clients' names and phone numbers, which you pick from Contacts or type in
  • Training sessions you check in, with optional notes
  • Payments you record (amount, date, method, note)
  • Prices, message templates, payment details you enter, and other settings

This data is shared between the app and its own home-screen widget on the same iPhone. It is not sent to the developer or to any third party.

Messages to your clients

Payment reminders are opened in WhatsApp or Messages for you to send. Once you tap Send, the message is handled by that app under its own privacy policy. Patrick PT itself doesn't send messages or contact anyone.

Bank SMS

If you set up the optional Shortcuts automation, the text of your bank's payment SMS is handed to Patrick PT on your iPhone, which reads the amount and sender to suggest which client paid. The text is kept on your device only until you confirm or dismiss it. The app cannot read your messages or bank notifications in any other way.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in
  • No servers: the developer never receives your data
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking, and no third-party SDKs
  • No access to your full contact list: the system contact picker gives the app only the contact you choose

Permissions

  • Notifications: to tell you when a client is due to pay, or when a payment SMS arrived.

Deletion

You can delete any client, session or payment in the app. Deleting a client removes their sessions and payments too. Deleting the app removes its data from the device. Data in your iCloud can be removed in iPhone Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
