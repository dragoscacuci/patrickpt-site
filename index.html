Common questions

Does the app send messages to my clients by itself?

No. When a client is due to pay, you get a notification. Tapping it opens WhatsApp or Messages with the reminder already written, and you tap Send. iPhone apps can't send messages for you in the background.

When is a client due?

After the number of sessions or the number of weeks set in Settings (12 sessions or 4 weeks by default), whichever comes first. Each client can have their own numbers. Sessions done before they pay count toward the next subscription, which starts on the day they pay.

How do I connect my bank's payment SMS?

In the app, open Settings → BT payment SMS (Shortcuts) and follow the five steps. It sets up a Shortcuts automation that hands each payment SMS to the app, which then asks you which client paid.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone and in your own private iCloud, so it survives a new phone. There is no account and no server.
